# NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge: 3-Minute Video Walkthrough Script

**Speaker:** Candidate (Growth Intern Applicant)  
**Target Audience:** Prashanth A R (Growth Lead) & NxtWave Growth Hiring Team  
**Format:** Loom / Screen Share + Face Cam  
**Total Target Duration:** 2 minutes 55 seconds (under 3:00 limit)  

---

## Video Production & Delivery Tips Before Recording:
- **Energy Level:** High, confident, builder mindset. Speak conversationally as if you're presenting a sprint review to your team lead.
- **Visual Setup:** Split-screen Loom (Face camera bottom-left/right, browser open with `index.html` full screen).
- **Pacing:** Crisp, brisk, no rambling. Practice once with a timer before recording your final take.

---

## The Timed Script (Word-for-Word)

### [0:00 – 0:30] Part 1: The Hook, Persona & The ₹2,000 Math Reality
**(Visual: Face on Camera full screen or split-screen. Direct eye contact.)**

> "Hi Prashanth and the NxtWave team! When I looked at this challenge—getting 500 final-year engineering students to register with just ₹2,000 in 7 days—the first thing I did was discard the standard edtech playbook.
> 
> Here's the raw math: With a ₹2,000 budget, running Meta or Google ads at an average ₹25 CPC will get you around 70 clicks and barely 15 registrations. That's a 97% failure rate.
> 
> So who are we targeting? We are targeting final-year B.Tech students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges. Their biggest fear right now is the 'blank resume'—hiring freezes are real, placement drives are around the corner, and their college curriculum hasn't taught them a single practical AI tool. 
> 
> Our hook isn't 'learn theory'—it's: *'Build a live AI web app in 60 minutes, deploy it to a public URL, and put 3 verified bullet points on your resume today.'*"

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] Part 2: The 3-Channel Strategy & Distribution Economics
**(Visual: Switch to Growth Dashboard on `index.html` or Slide 2 of Growth Plan.)**

> "To guarantee 500+ registrations, I prioritized three channels based on ground-level trust rather than ad spend:
> 
> **Channel 1 is our College CR Micro-Bounty Program:** Class Representatives hold 100% trust in unofficial batch WhatsApp groups. I allocated ₹1,200 into twelve ₹100 Amazon vouchers for active CRs who get 25 students from their class to register. That brings in **312 registrations at ₹3.84 CPR**.
> 
> **Channel 2 is our Peer-to-Peer Viral Milestone Loop:** Engineering students prepare in tight friend groups. By unlocking tangible placement assets at 1, 3, and 5 friend referrals—like GitHub starter repos and ATS prompt packs—we achieve a viral K-factor of 0.38, yielding **146 registrations**.
> 
> **Channel 3 is High-Signal Tech Communities:** Sharing free, open-source ATS checker scripts on Reddit's `r/Btechtards` and placement Telegrams, driving another **80 registrations at ₹0 cost**.
> 
> Total outcome: **538 validated registrations at a blended CPR of ₹3.62**, leaving a ₹50 buffer on our ₹2,000 budget."

---

### [1:15 – 2:15] Part 3: Live Working Asset Demonstration
**(Visual: Full screen on browser showing `index.html`. Interact with the features live.)**

> "Instead of just building a generic landing page, I built a functional growth engine to test this loop. Let me show you how it works:
> 
> First, here is the **Branch-Specific AI Project Matcher** *(click 'ECE/EEE' or 'Mechanical' on screen)*. If an ECE or Mechanical student lands here, they often think AI isn't for them. But when they click their branch, it dynamically generates their exact 60-minute build, the tech stack using Gemini API, and previews the three resume bullet points they'll earn.
> 
> When they submit the 15-second registration form *(click Submit, confetti bursts)*, it immediately opens the **Viral Milestone Hub**. Look at this: their unique referral link is ready, and as friends join *(click 'Simulate Friend Joining')*, the progress bar fills, unlocking Tier 1 GitHub code, Tier 2 ATS prompts, and Tier 3 VIP 1-on-1 code reviews.
> 
> And to solve the notorious 70% edtech workshop drop-off, look at Tab 2: the **WhatsApp Automation Simulator** *(click WhatsApp tab)*. It models the exact 4-touchpoint retention drip: instant ticket pass, 24-hour zero-setup Colab link to kill environment friction, 1-hour live Zoom reminder, and post-session certificate delivery."

---

### [2:15 – 2:45] Part 4: How I Thought & What I Deliberately Rejected
**(Visual: Face camera split-screen with AI Learning Notes.)**

> "A major part of growth is knowing what *not* to do, especially when using AI.
> 
> When I prompted AI for ideas, it gave me two dangerously flawed suggestions:
> 1. It suggested running Meta ads and giving away an iPad. On a ₹2,000 budget, that completely destroys credibility and budget in 24 hours. I rejected that and built the CR incentive network instead.
> 2. It suggested teaching a complex LangChain and local vector DB RAG pipeline. That would crash on 4GB RAM college laptops and cause an 80% workshop drop-off. I rejected it and scoped a cloud-first, zero-setup Gemini API app on Streamlit Cloud that any student can deploy in 40 minutes."

### [2:45 – 3:00] Part 5: Closing & Why NxtWave
**(Visual: Face camera full screen, confident and enthusiastic.)**

> "In 7 days, this campaign doesn't just collect emails—it activates real campus champions, delivers genuine placement value, and scales a repeatable playbook.
> 
> I love shipping fast, experimenting with real users, and turning messy problems into clean growth systems. I'd love to bring this exact bias for action to NxtWave as a Growth Intern. Thank you!"
