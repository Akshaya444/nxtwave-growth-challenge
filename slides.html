Slide 5 • Thinking & Learnings

Critical Judgment: What I Rejected & Why

Demonstrating learnability, ownership, and human-in-the-loop decision making.

Rejected #1

Meta / Instagram Ads

Why: Spending ₹2,000 on Instagram ads yields only ~16 signups at ₹25 CPC (97% failure).

Pivoted to: WhatsApp CR micro-incentives (312 signups at ₹3.84 CPR).

Rejected #2

LangChain RAG Pipeline

Why: Complex local Docker/vector DB setup crashes on 4GB RAM college laptops.

Pivoted to: Zero-install Gemini API app on Streamlit Cloud (100% completion rate).

Rejected #3

"Win an iPad" Sweepstakes

Why: Unbelievable on a ₹2,000 budget; college students ignore fake lucky draws.

Pivoted to: Deterministic academic perks (GitHub repos, ATS prompts, VIP reviews).
